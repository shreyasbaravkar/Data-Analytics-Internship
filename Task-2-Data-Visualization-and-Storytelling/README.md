# 📊 Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Objective
Create interactive visualizations that communicate meaningful business insights using Tableau.

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Sample - Superstore**
- Contains sales, profit, customer, product, and regional information.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau Desktop
- GitHub

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Total Sales by Category
- Compares total sales across product categories.
- Helps identify the highest-performing category.

### 2. Total Profit by Region
- Displays profit generated in each region.
- Highlights the most and least profitable regions.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend
- Shows sales performance over time.
- Helps identify seasonal trends and business growth.

### 4. Top Sales by Sub-Category
- Highlights the best-selling product sub-categories.
- Useful for product performance analysis.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology generates the highest sales among product categories.
- The West region contributes the highest overall profit.
- Sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating seasonal demand.
- Top-performing sub-categories contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- Business should focus on strengthening low-performing regions while maintaining high-performing categories.
